Our Workshop

A garage built
by riders, for riders.

Bike House started in 2014 as a single-bay workshop on the Horana road. A decade on, we're Sri Lanka's go-to specialist for high-capacity machines — and we still answer the phone ourselves.

From Panigale valve services to MT-09 ECU flashes, from accident rebuilds to custom supermoto conversions, we work on bikes most workshops politely refuse. Every job is documented, every customer is on a first-name basis, and every bike leaves better than it came in.
