Our Story

From one bench
to a regional landmark.

Bike House started in 2014 when Supun Chaturanga and his younger brother Kasun opened a single-bay workshop on the Horana road. A decade on, we're Sri Lanka's go-to specialist for high-capacity machines — and we still answer the phone ourselves.

We never set out to be a brand. We set out to do work that owners trust — and to be the kind of place where you can walk in, point at your bike, and have a real conversation about what's actually wrong with it. Everything you see today is built on that.

The mural on the back wall — a tribute to WSBK rider Michael van der Mark — was painted by a customer who rebuilt his Yamaha R1 here twice in three years. That's the kind of relationship we run on.
